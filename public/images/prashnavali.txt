All of these Prashnavali are dedicated to Lord Shri Rama, Lord Hanumana, Lord Ganesha and Goddess Durga, respectively. Therefore, any devotee who, with faith and devotion, remembers these deities while asking a question, certainly receives appropriate guidance. Whenever you find yourself in a state of indecision, you can make the right choice by sincerely and faithfully seeking help from the following Prashnavali.

Rama Shalaka - The Rama Shalaka Prashnavali is found in the revered scripture Shri Ramcharitmanas. Composed by Goswami Tulsidas and enriched with divine Chaupais, this sacred Prashnavali serves as a spiritual tool to aid individuals in making important decisions with clarity and faith.
Hanuman Prashnavali - The Hanuman Prashnavali is also known as the Hanuman Trikona Prashnavali. This sacred oracle is dedicated to Lord Hanuman and is described in the astrological text known as Hanuman Jyotisha.
Ganesha Prashnavali - This Prashnavali dedicated to Lord Ganesha consists of 64 numbers, through which the seeker receives answers to their questions. According to traditional beliefs, this Prashnavali helps devotees make decisions with the guidance of Lord Ganapati.
Durga Prashnavali - Durga Prashnavali dedicated to Goddess Durga is highly popular among Her devotees. It is also known as the Navadurga Prashnavali. Through this Prashnavali, Goddess Durga provides divine guidance to Her devotees.

In Hinduism, popular Prashnavali such as the Rama Shalaka, Hanuman Prashnavali, Ganesha Prashnavali and Durga Prashnavali are given for the convenience of Drik Panchang users. According to one's devotion, any of these Prashnavali can be used to find answers to questions or doubts lingering in the mind. Since ancient times, these Prashnavali have served as a means of guidance for mankind and remain widely practiced even today.
