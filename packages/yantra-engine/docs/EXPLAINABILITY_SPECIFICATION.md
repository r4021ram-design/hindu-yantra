# SGOS Explainability Engine Specification

**Package Scope**: `@yantra/engine` / Hindu Panchang Application

---

## 1. Overview & Architectural Role

The **Explainability Engine** bridges raw computational geometry and traditional Shastric knowledge. It provides a transparent, evidence-oriented breakdown of why every line, triangle, and layer exists in a Yantra.

---

## 2. 3-Tier Evidence Classification Taxonomy

Every citation, geometry proof, and symbolism claim is assigned to one of three evidence classification tiers:

| Evidence Tier Badge | Classification Category | Description | Citation Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 📜 **Canonical / Scripture-Backed** | Primary Textual Canon | Explicit verses in recognized Tantric scriptures with chapter/text citations. | *Soundarya Lahari 11*, *Yogini Hridaya II.14* |
| 📿 **Traditional Lineage-Specific** | Sampradaya Oral Tradition | Unbroken lineage commentaries and ritual manuals without explicit root verse numbers. | *Nityotsava*, *Setubandha Commentary* |
| 🔬 **Research Interpretation** | Modern Geometric Hypothesis | Analytical reconstructions and mathematical symmetry hypotheses clearly demarcated. | *PHI Golden Ratio Sub-Triangle Symmetry Hypothesis* |
