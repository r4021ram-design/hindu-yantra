# SGKB Editorial AI Guidelines

**Package Scope**: `@yantra/engine` / Hindu Panchang Application  
**Specification Version**: 1.0.0-sgkb.editorial-ai-guidelines  

---

## 1. Principles of AI-Assisted Editorial Tools

1. **AI as Assistant, Not Author**: AI tools act strictly as copilot drafting assistants to human scholars.
2. **AI Provenance Audit Trail**: Every AI draft maintains complete provenance (`generatedAt`, `generatedByModel`, `promptVersion`, `reviewStatus`).
3. **No Fabrication Zero-Tolerance**: AI engines MUST NEVER invent or fabricate scriptural verses or citations.
4. **Operation Analytics Tracking**: Editorial Boards monitor suggestion acceptance/rejection ratios via the Editorial Analytics Dashboard.
