## 1. Metadata Field Contracts

Each Yantra record maintains rich metadata fields:
- **Deity**: Presiding divine aspect (e.g. *Lalita Tripurasundari*, *Ganesha*, *Kuber*).
- **Mantra**: Authentic Beej Mantra string for japa and meditation.
- **Traditional Usage**: Primary spiritual and practical applications.
- **Historical Period**: Earliest recorded textual epoch (e.g. *c. 8th Century CE*).
- **Evidence Tier**: `canonical` 📜, `traditional` 📿, or `research` 🔬.
- **Confidence Level**: `High`, `Moderate`, or `Speculative`.
- **Related Yantras**: Cross-linking array connecting geometrically or functionally related Yantras.
